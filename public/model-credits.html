Playtest 06 · the people behind the props

Good finds. Generous makers.

This alpha uses real authored 3D meshes and skeletal character animation. These model licenses permit free use; the three CC BY models require attribution. Model files and licenses are bundled locally.

Quaternius · CC0

Modern, animated street characters

Ultimate Modular Characters and Ultimate Modular Women supply the five adult human rigs and 24 animation clips per rig. Casual Hoodie becomes the diver; Punk becomes Wired Eddie; Casual 2 becomes Officer Grudge and Clive; Casual Woman becomes Mabel. Recolored, given a shared brush material, and fitted with original skinned aprons, cap, badge, belt and radio. Existing animation clips are mapped to gameplay; no new motion capture is claimed.

Obtained as data assets from a public mirror at commit db3df04d1e4714298a09510b26fb6de6645138a2 after the author's download quota was exceeded. The pack's own CC0 license files are included in assets/models/v6.

Poly by Google · CC BY 3.0

Skunk

Original skunk mesh by Google, preserved with its texture. Adapted in Blender with an original six-bone skeleton, weighted legs and tail, and eight simple animation clips. Scaled and given the common brush material. Source attribution, license and this modification notice must accompany redistributed builds.

Kay Lousberg · CC0

KayKit — Adventurers

Legacy v5 Rogue character, retained in the asset folder but not loaded by v6, with its original skeleton and animation clips. Fantasy weapons and cape hidden; clothing recolored to ochre. Scaled and fitted with scavenged equipment. Source commit: 672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47.

Quaternius · CC0

Ultimate Animated Animals

Fox, plus legacy Shiba Inu and Husky files retained but not loaded by v6, distributed by the author at https://poly.pizza/bundle/Animated-Animal-Pack-ILAPXeUYiS. Scaled and connected to movement, attacks, hit reactions and nonlethal knockout behavior.

Kenney · CC0

Furniture, City Commercial, City Industrial, Nature, Car, Food and Survival Kits

Selected vehicles, roof details, scenery, containers, food and household props. The v6 shops use original facade geometry and shallow display-room interiors; older Kenney buildings remain bundled. Scaled, repositioned and occasionally tinted. Original pack license files and dependent texture maps are included.

Quaternius · CC0

Trash Container

Authored dumpster. Lid separated in Blender and given a hinge for the search animation; normalized to container sizes.

CreativeTrio · CC0

Closed Umbrella

Scaled and attached to the character hand. A custom opening-canopy effect is added for the finishing swing.

CreativeTrio · CC0

Toolbox

Scaled for found loot and the carried stack.

Plewr · CC0

Goose

Blender source baked to a static glTF mesh, validated and given a porcelain material. Used as decorative loot, not an enemy.

Poly by Google · CC BY 3.0

Shopping cart

Scaled, grounded, recolored to muted copper and sage, and adapted into the trailing loot trolley. This attribution and license link must travel with redistributed builds.

Jarlan Perez · CC BY 3.0

Broom

Scaled, grounded, recolored in warm wood and straw, and attached to the animated hand. This attribution and license link must travel with redistributed builds.

Original game work

Gameplay, UI, adaptation, lighting, windowed shop facades and interiors, canvas backpack, sack, woven basket, broken bottle, sock, battery sock, loose batteries, baton, painted street surfaces, interactive jazz and sound synthesis. The paper-stack viewer is a separate original experiment. The painterly card illustrations and texture atlas were generated for this project; their prompts and process are recorded in assets/ART-PROVENANCE.txt, assets/LOOT-ART-PROVENANCE.txt and assets/V6-ART-PROVENANCE.txt. Card illustrations remain stylized interpretations of the finds.

Three.js 0.180.0 and its GLTFLoader, SkeletonUtils and BufferGeometryUtils are included under the MIT license. Addon module imports were adapted for this build’s local script loading.

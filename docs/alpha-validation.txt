DUMPSTER DIVER / PLAYTEST 06 / VALIDATION / 13 OCTOBER 2026

PASS: 33 automated checks (node --test tests/*.cjs).
Includes 1,000 distinct raid seeds with constant item budgets, ordinary route
floors, capped top-tier clustering, guaranteed crafting resources and no rerolls.
Every enemy category has a permanent-defeat test. Managers also have window
suspicion, decay, door emergence and defeat checks. Police investigate noise;
skunks warn, spray, slow and can be interrupted. Crafting consumes materials
exactly once on completion, preserves them on cancellation and cannot duplicate
an equipped weapon. Rummaging keeps the clock/enemies live and contact cancels it.

The full-raid simulation starts at the normal spawn with every enemy active.
It uses movement/pathfinding, search and attack actions, without teleporting,
injected loot, extended time or changed damage. Seed 7 collects cans, sock,
lamp and batteries; defeats Officer Grudge and Patch; crafts the heavy sock;
and extracts 5 points. This is simulation coverage, not a claim that the whole
sequence was manually completed through the browser.

PASS: 71 bundled glTF binaries have valid headers, mesh references and local
buffer/image dependencies. Every model/script/stylesheet reference checked in
the game entry page resolves. All game JavaScript parses. Model hashes and
source/license records are included in assets/models/manifest.json.

BROWSER OBSERVATIONS
Actual new animated characters, skunks, custom bags, scenery and item art load.
Shop display objects are visible through windows. Mabel responds to proximity,
opens her door and comes outside. Broom hits and counters reduce her health.
Loot search, auto-equipped basket/pan, carried finds, live interruptions,
knocked-loose-item recovery and paused kit inspection work in the inspected runs.
A browser run extracted a lamp with basket and pan equipped (4 haul points).
The large rummage panel blurs the world while the timer and enemies continue.
The separate viewer switches subjects and compares painted 3D to paper layers.
No browser console errors/warnings were reported in inspected v6 game sessions
or the final paper viewer. Missing mesh normals were repaired at import; the
previously dark broom was visually verified with correct wood/straw lighting.
Street rendering sampled around 60 fps at 1280x720 on this machine.

LIMITS
Browser playtests did not complete the new crafting-to-extraction sequence;
that is covered by the full-raid simulation above. Audio scheduling is checked;
the procedural mix has not had a dedicated listening pass in this iteration.
Other hardware, full mobile controls and multiplayer are unverified. The paper
look is a static front-cutout experiment, not a finished animated renderer.
This is a playable alpha, with further visual, animation and balance work ahead.

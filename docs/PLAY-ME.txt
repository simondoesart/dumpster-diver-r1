DUMPSTER DIVER / ONE MORE BIN / PLAYTEST 06

PLAY
http://127.0.0.1:8772/
Desktop browser with WebGL 2. Sound starts when you enter the game.
If the local preview stops, serve this folder with:
  npm start
Keep the entire folder together. Assets and sound work offline once served.
This is a solo alpha. Hosting it lets people play separate local runs.

THE NIGHT'S JOB
Rummage around Brimble Lane, carry home strange treasures, and reach the van
before the three-minute collection deadline. Aim for 12 points, or extract
early with any nonempty haul. Only your personal best persists between runs.

CONTROLS
WASD / arrows: move relative to the screen.
Click ground to walk; click a container, loose item or van to walk and interact.
E: search, pick up or load the van. Press once; no need to hold.
Space: tap or hold to swing. Third swing is a finishing attack.
Shift: dodge, then hold to hurry. F: hop. Q: drop the selected find.
C: assemble the night-night sock when carrying a sock and batteries.
M: overview. Click the small map to navigate. Mouse wheel / +/-: zoom.
Tab / I: inspect your kit. Escape or Back to the block closes it.
Drag the character in your kit to rotate it.
Escape: cancel searching/crafting, close a found-item reveal, or otherwise pause.
The Sound button mutes or restores the entire soundscape.

WHAT CHANGED
Five modern, authored human character rigs with skeletal animation, including
Wired Eddie, Officer Grudge and two shop managers. Shared gouache material,
custom aprons and police accessories. Dogs have become animated skunks.
Layered building facades, deep window frames, visible display-room contents,
shop signs and hinged doors. Standing close to windows builds suspicion;
back away to cool it down, or the manager comes outside. These are shallow
window interiors; you cannot walk into the shops in this alpha.
Original detailed canvas rucksack, cloth sack, woven basket and new weapons.
Loot flies into the pack and visibly stacks/wobbles as you move.
A large, live rummage view with blurred street, container-specific packing,
passing unrelated shapes, and an actual find revealed only after completion.
A seeded raid with 24 predetermined finds across eight containers.
A separate art-lab.html compares painted 3D and a static stacked-paper look.

THE LOCALS
Patch the fox and Wired Eddie can steal finds. Eddie also grabs loose items.
Skunks telegraph a spray, leaving a short-lived cloud that slows you and
interrupts rummaging. Dodge away or counter before the spray.
Officer Grudge investigates noisy rummaging and weapon swings.
Mabel and Clive become suspicious when you linger near their windows or doors.
Every threat has health and can be permanently knocked out for this raid.
A fresh raid restores everyone. Counters and third hits add damage; knocked
back enemies may need to be approached again before you can finish a combo.
Stolen finds drop when a thief is hit or defeated. Follow a thief to its stash
or retrieve loose items with E. Dropped food/radios can distract animals.
The fighting is cartoon slapstick with non-graphic knockouts.

GEAR AND CRAFTING
Rucksack: 6 spaces. Basket: 8. Sack: 10. Trolley: 12. Upgrades do not use space.
Heavy finds use two spaces and slow you a little. No carrier downgrade.
Start with a broom. Find a pan (2 damage), umbrella (reach), or broken bottle
(fast, short range, 2 damage). Gear auto-equips for the current raid.
Find a sock and loose batteries in containers; press C to make the heavy sock
(2 damage, sweeping reach). Assembly takes 2.2 live seconds. Moving, dodging,
being interrupted or cancelling keeps the ingredients. Completion consumes
one of each ingredient and equips the weapon, freeing both carry spaces.

LOOT AND FAIRNESS
A fresh run creates its seed and fixes the complete container contents.
The total item pool is identical across seeds; placement and order vary.
Every container starts with ordinary scoring loot. Mabel's second layer has
a basket. Sock, batteries and bottle are spread across the three early street
containers. No top-tier find starts in Mabel's bin; at most one per container.
The reel is decorative and does not determine rewards. Cancelling cannot reroll.
This reduces empty-route and jackpot-cluster luck; it does not prove equal
travel effort or threat exposure. Those need playtest balancing. Client-side
seeds are suitable for this solo alpha, not tamper-proof multiplayer fairness.
For a repeatable test run, append &seed=7 to the play URL (base-36 seed).

LIVE SEARCH, PAUSED INSPECTION
Enemies, clock and music continue during rummaging and crafting. Background
blur intentionally limits vision. Move, dodge or attack to abandon a search.
Names, points and rarity are withheld until the find is secured. Better finds
get richer musical flourishes. Searching does not protect you from theft.
Tab opens a paused solo inspection screen with your actual inventory, equipment,
space grid and rotatable character. Opening it cancels an unfinished action.
The same screen shows your findings after extraction or failure.

SOUND
Original synthesized jazz: electric piano, plucked bass, brushed percussion,
footstep hi-hats, ambience, container sounds and rarity-dependent loot phrases.
Threat proximity strengthens the footstep percussion; the final stretch adds
tempo and percussion. New reel ticks, manager warning, spray and crafting cues.
This remains a procedural alpha score with room for further listening/mix work.

PAPER TEST
http://127.0.0.1:8772/art-lab.html
Select a character/prop, switch between Painted 3D and Stacked paper, and turn it.
Paper mode bakes a front-view pose onto 14 layered cutouts. It is deliberately
a separate static visual test. Full animation and side views would need more
artwork or a hybrid mesh approach; it is not the game's current renderer.

VALIDATION AND SCOPE
Automated checks: node --test tests/*.cjs
They cover the physical loop, inventory, gear, combat, defeats, extraction,
manager emergence, skunk spray, police noise response, live interruptions,
crafting completion/cancellation and 1,000 seeded loot distributions.
Browser checks include the new rigs, painterly scene, window display contents,
manager emergence, counters/hit reactions, rummaging and interruptions, carried
loot, gear discoveries, inspection and the separate paper viewer.
Observed street rendering reached around 60 fps at 1280x720 on this machine;
other hardware is unverified. See VALIDATION-v6.txt for the final checked scope.
No economy, wallet, account, stash, backend or networked players in this alpha.
See README.md for deployment instructions and the current scope.
